FAQ

Questions you may have

What exactly do you build?

Systems that make you money with the newest AI available: speed-to-lead calling, follow-up sequences, a phone receptionist, back-office automations and custom tools. Each one is picked for your specific leaks, then wired into your business.

Do I have to change my phone system, CRM or calendar?

No. We build on top of what you already run. Your number, your CRM and your calendar stay.

How fast do I see results?

Reactivation and speed-to-lead show up in the first weeks, because they work on leads you already have. Everything is tracked, so you read the numbers yourself.

What does it cost?

A setup fee plus a monthly retainer, sized on your call and lead volume. We quote it on the call, once we know what the leaks are costing you.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Every system is trained on your services, prices and rules, tested before launch, and hands off to a human the moment something goes beyond its scope.

Can it sound like my company?

Voice, greeting, vocabulary and tone are built from how your team actually talks. Your customers hear your company.

What happens after launch?

We monitor it, report on it and tune it monthly. You are not left alone with a robot.

Can I turn it off?

Anytime. Nothing about your phone system or your data is locked in.

My business is unusual. Will this fit?

The first call is a diagnosis, not a pitch. We map how leads and jobs actually move through your business and build around that.

What do you measure?

Answer rate, time to first call, booked jobs, revived leads, hours of admin removed. You get the numbers, not a feeling.

Will it keep up as I grow?

The same system takes ten calls a day or ten at once. New locations and new lines are configuration, not a rebuild.

Why not just hire someone instead?

Hire for judgment. For picking up in under a second at 2am and chasing every lead forever, a human was never the right tool.
