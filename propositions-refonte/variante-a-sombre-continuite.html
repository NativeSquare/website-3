FAQ

Questions you may have

What exactly does NativeSquare do?

We build and run AI voice agents that answer your calls, book your appointments and follow up with your leads — plugged into your existing phone number, calendar and CRM.

How do you make sure the AI doesn't mess up a conversation?

Every agent is trained on your services, prices and policies, tested against real scenarios before launch, and hands off to a human the moment a call goes beyond its scope.

Do I need to change my phone system or CRM?

No. We plug into what you already use — your number stays the same, your CRM stays the same.

How much does this cost?

A one-time setup fee plus a monthly retainer, sized to your call volume. We price it on a call, after we understand what missed calls currently cost you.

What ROI should I expect, and how fast?

Most clients see the effect within the first weeks: calls that used to go to voicemail become booked appointments. We track everything so you can read the numbers yourself.

Do you offer support and training?

Yes. Setup, team training and ongoing support are part of every engagement, with a monthly optimization pass.

Can I customize how the AI sounds and speaks?

Voice, tone, greeting, vocabulary — all of it is tailored to your business. Your customers hear your company, not a robot.

What if I want to go back to human-only calls?

You can, anytime. Nothing about your phone system is locked in.

How does this handle the specific challenges of my business?

Every deployment starts with a discovery call where we map your actual call flows and pain points. The agent is built around them, not from a template.

What measurable improvements can I expect?

Answer rate, time-to-answer, booked appointments, after-hours captures, follow-up completion — all measured, all reported.

How does it integrate with our current systems?

Through native integrations with the main CRMs, calendars and phone systems — and custom connections when needed.

Will it scale as we grow?

The same agent handles ten calls a day or ten at the same time. Adding locations, lines or languages is configuration, not a rebuild.
